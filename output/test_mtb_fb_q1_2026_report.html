Methodology
ASCM
Augmented Synthetic Control Method. Builds a weighted combination of holdout DMAs to create a synthetic treatment counterfactual, with ridge-augmented bias correction.
BSTS
Bayesian Structural Time Series. Models the counterfactual using a state-space time series with spike-and-slab variable selection on control DMAs.
DiD
Difference-in-Differences. Compares pre-to-post changes in treatment vs. holdout groups to estimate the causal effect, controlling for time trends.
The final estimate is a weighted ensemble of all three methods. Weights are assigned based on pre-period fit quality (L2 imbalance, R²) and cross-validation performance. Results are validated with placebo tests, AA balance checks, and estimator agreement analysis.
